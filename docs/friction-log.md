# Friction log

The most tedious part was finding and removing the AWS Organizations policies that were blocking my agentic workflow. Two service control policies (SCPs), p-39p6wt1g and then p-p10l2788, explicitly denied CloudFormation, Lambda, DynamoDB, API Gateway, SSM and ECR. Those are the core services needed to deploy the backend and the AI coach agent. Only the second one showed up after I removed the first, so I had to hunt them down one at a time.

As a first-time user, I found the location hard to reach. It wasn’t obvious that the FireFIT project had created a separate member account inside an organization, or that the fix had to be made from a different account, the management account. The CLI error gave the policy’s ID (ARN) but not where to change it. Getting there meant signing out, signing back in as the management account, then going to AWS Organizations › Policies › Service control policies, and finally to the FireFIT account’s Policies tab. When I first tried to open those settings, the console said I didn’t have access, even though I own the entire AWS account.

An agent-guided onboarding flow would help. At each sign-in, it could ask what you’re trying to accomplish, then take you straight to the settings you need, or let you skip ahead. At minimum, an access-denied error caused by a policy should name the account that controls it and link straight to that policy.
