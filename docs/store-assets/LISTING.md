# Amazon Appstore listing kit (Fire TV)

On the Fire TV home screen, an app's **cover background**, **tile** and **short description** come from its Amazon Appstore listing. A sideloaded APK only provides its launcher art: Fire OS shows the app's square **icon** in "Recently used apps" (centred in the tile), so the icon carries the full FireFIT wordmark; the 16:9 **banner** (the app-card art) is declared too, for launchers that use it. Upload these in the [Amazon Developer Console](https://developer.amazon.com/apps-and-games) when publishing.

## Title
FireFIT TV

## Short description (the brief text on the Fire TV cover)
FireFIT TV transforms your television into an AI-powered personal fitness trainer, using your smartphone's camera to track exercises, count repetitions, and provide real-time form feedback. With interactive workouts, personalized progress tracking, and estimated calories burned, FireFIT makes fitness accessible, engaging, and equipment-free.

## Long description
FireFIT turns your TV into an AI form mirror: on-device computer vision tracks 33 body points and draws a live skeleton over your video, with AR-style real-time form highlights. Your phone is the camera, so there's nothing extra to buy.

• No extra hardware: scan a QR code and your phone becomes the camera. No app install needed on the phone.
• Real-time tracking: on-device pose tracking counts only full-range reps, times planks only while your body is straight, and flags form issues like caving knees or sagging hips.
• A complete session in about 11 minutes: squats, push-ups and a plank, with rests, tips and a live HUD designed for the couch and the remote.
• An AI coach that changes your plan: after every workout, a coach built on Amazon Bedrock reviews your history, tells you what went well and what to fix, reads its report aloud, and sets your next session.
• Track your progress: lifetime stats, day streaks and an after-action report for every past session.
• Private by design: your camera video stays on your home network. Only workout numbers are saved.
• Household profiles: everyone keeps their own history and coach.

Calorie figures are estimates. FireFIT provides training feedback, not medical advice.
